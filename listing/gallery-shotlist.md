# Gallery shot list — Tiered Iron Chests 1.0.0

Global frame rules (all shots): 1920×1080 PNG, captured in-game (F2), vanilla visuals with no shader packs, GUI scale 2, neutral daylight or even interior lighting, resource pack off. Shot 1 is the listing hero and thumbnail source; keep its center third free of HUD or text overlays.

## 1. Hero — the whole progression, open
**Frame:** five chests — copper, iron, gold, diamond, netherite — in one evenly spaced row, every lid open, every GUI open so the slot grids read left to right: 27 → 54 → 72 → 108 → 108. Camera at chest height, slight three-quarter angle, tier metals visibly tinted darkest to brightest. No HUD (F1). Crop-safe for square icon re-use.
**Conversion rationale:** the entire pitch in one frame — a searcher for "iron chests" instantly sees the familiar progression *plus* more slots than vanilla. The hero shot earns the click; slot-count legibility is the hook.

## 2. Upgrade kit — contents preserved
**Frame:** two-panel composition. Panel A: crafting table UI with a loaded iron chest plus the gold kit material in the grid, output slot showing the gold chest. Panel B: the resulting gold chest opened, holding the exact same item stacks. Stacks must be identifiable in both panels.
**Conversion rationale:** upgrade kits are the core mechanic and "do I lose my stuff?" is the #1 adoption question. Showing items surviving the upgrade answers it without a single word.

## 3. Automation parity — hoppers, minecart, comparator
**Frame:** one live survival scene: a hopper line feeding a diamond chest, a hopper minecart on rails above unloading into it, and a comparator + redstone lamp reading its fill level. All wiring visible and tidy; must be a functional capture, not staged.
**Conversion rationale:** automation compatibility is the make-or-break feature for chest mods and the top FAQ. A live capture signals "works out of the box" and doubles as visual proof for the parity claim.

## 4. Vanilla-adjacent textures in a real base
**Frame:** an ordinary survival storage room — walls, lighting, mixed vanilla decor — with the five tiered chests integrated, lids closed, at natural render distance. No mod menu, no F3 overlay, nothing that breaks the "could be vanilla" illusion.
**Conversion rationale:** "fits my pack and my base" is a top adoption filter. This shot proves the one-model-five-skins art direction reads vanilla-adjacent, not cartoony — the objection a texture-skeptical player needs settled before installing.

## 5. Netherite blast test
**Frame:** netherite chest sitting unharmed in a fresh creeper crater, opened to show contents intact, scorch and rubble around it. Wide framing so the crater scale reads. Optional inset: the same chest pre-blast.
**Conversion rationale:** a memorable, screenshotable differentiator no legacy iron-chests page shows. It makes the netherite tier's blast resistance concrete and gives the gallery a shareable moment.
